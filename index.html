About me

I am a Senior Research Fellow(SRF) in the Nuclear Physics Division at the UGC-DAE CSR, Kolkata Centre under the supervision of Dr. Rajarshi Raut .

My research area is exploring nuclear structure at its most fundamental level, while maintaining a clear focus on its broader significance across various fields of applied science. Our group also focuses on the precision measurements at the microscopic level as well that probe and challenge the Standard Model of elementary particle interactions. In this context, I was part of the collaboration working on the VECC-INGA campaign 2023-2025 and our group played a crucial role in data acquisition (DAQ) support.

When I’m not in the lab, I enjoy spending time at the gym, cycling or traveling to discover inspiring landscapes and ancient architectures. I also enjoy cooking and sharing meals with the people I love.

Research

My thesis aims to study the nuclear structure of nuclei near shell closures, especially those around the doubly-magic 56Ni core. Both experimental methods—such as in-beam gamma-ray spectroscopy with large detector arrays—and theoretical approaches using large-scale shell model calculations will be used.

Nuclear excitations are the primary focus for this work, which leads us to understand the mechanism of generation of the angular momentum in nuclei. Recently, our paper on 69Ga nucleus was accepted in Physical Review C, in which we established the collective excitations builds upon the single particle excitation scheme.
